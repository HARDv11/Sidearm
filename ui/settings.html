Friends…

      

    
Your call signHow you sign to friends. They can rename you on their side.

    
Your avatarFriends see which one you picked: only its number travels, not the picture.

      

    
NameOnly what it's called in menus and hints. It works the same.

      

    
Unread on the start pageA block above the notes. Pinned first: friends you starred come first, everyone else when they haven't written.

      

    
Tabs beside the windowWhoever wrote gets a tab with their avatar at Sidearm's edge; the wing opens every chat. Against the left edge of the screen they move to the right.

    
Quick repliesReady answers on the card that pops up, up to 5, separated by |. Empty: the usual ones.

    
Delete RavenYour keys, friends and messages on this PC, and your inbox on the post office. Friends can't reach you until you add them again.

Translation

    
Translate pages intoAlt + T or the shield panel translates the page you're on (through Google Translate).

      

    
How pages are translatedExperimentalPage proxy: Google's translated copy of the page (links stay translated, sign-ins may not work). In place: the words change on the real page, so you stay signed in (a bold or linked word inside a sentence is translated on its own).

      

    
Pages in other languagesOffer: a Translate button appears once per site. Always: translated as soon as they open.

      

    
Languages you readNever offered for translation. Codes, comma separated: uk, en, pl…

Feel

    
Ghost mode keySee-through and click-through, so a map or guide stays up while you play. Only taken while Sidearm is on screen.

    
Ghost opacityHow see-through ghost mode is.

    
Scroll joystickHold the middle button on a page and move: the further, the faster, any way. A click leaves it on until the next click. Links still open a Glance.

    
Joystick speedThe wheel changes it too while the joystick is on.

    
Joystick keyStarts it where the pointer is, for a laptop's touchpad. Again or Esc stops it.

      

    
Mute new pagesNo surprise audio over game comms. Alt + M or the speaker unmutes a tab.

    
Clean linksCopied links lose utm_*, fbclid, gclid and other trackers; Google and Facebook redirects are unwrapped.

    
Amnesia modeEvery holster wipes history, cookies, site data and recent searches, and tabs aren't kept between sessions. Signs you out of everything.

Speed & sleep

    
Keep a tab readyOne blank page engine waits while Sidearm is drawn, so new tabs open instantly. About 20–30 MB, freed when you holster.

    
Warm startWhen Sidearm starts with Windows, start its engine in the background and let it sleep, so the first draw is quick too.

    
How long a page took, and how much of that was Sidearm, is in the shield panel under “Page load”.
